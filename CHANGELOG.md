## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- `parseAddress`: Colombian urban addresses (Calle, Carrera, Avenida, Diagonal, Transversal, Circular...), letters, bis, quadrants, named avenues and complements.
- `formatAddress` / `normalizeAddress` with `long`, `short` and `code` styles.
- `isSameAddress` and `addressKey` to compare and deduplicate addresses.
- `isValidAddress`.
